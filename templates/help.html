How to use BizBridge

Quick answers for entrepreneurs, mentors, investors and administrators.

Quick start

  1. Register with your name, email, a password of at least 8 characters and your role (Entrepreneur, Mentor or Investor).
  2. Complete your profile. Entrepreneurs add business name, sector, stage and location. Mentors add expertise, availability and sectors. Investors add organisation, investor type, sectors and website.
  3. Check your recommendations on the dashboard. They are based on matching sector, stage and location.
  4. Search for people and opportunities and filter by sector, location, stage and type.
  5. Connect. Send a connection request, a mentorship request or an investor introduction. Once it is accepted you can send messages.

Frequently asked questions

Who can I search for?

Entrepreneurs can search for mentors, investors and other entrepreneurs (potential partners). Mentors and investors can search for entrepreneurs and their businesses.

Why can't I message someone?

Messaging opens only after a connection, mentorship or investor request has been accepted. This protects members from unwanted messages.

What does the verified badge mean?

An administrator has reviewed that mentor's or investor's profile. Even so, always do your own checks before you share money or private information.

Why did an opportunity disappear?

Opportunities are hidden automatically after their deadline has passed.

I forgot my password.

Use Forgot your password? on the login page. We email you a one-time link.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Edit profile → Delete my account. Your profile, requests and messages are permanently removed, as required by Rwanda's data protection law (Law No. 058/2021).

My account says it is suspended.

An administrator suspended the account, possibly after a complaint. Contact the BizBridge team so they can review it and reactivate the account.

Role guides

Entrepreneurs: keep your sector and stage up to date to get better matches. Request mentorship with a clear topic.

Mentors: answer mentorship and connection requests from Mentorship requests and Connections.

Investors: search businesses by sector, location and stage, then send connection requests or answer introductions.

Administrators: use the Admin console to verify mentors and investors, suspend or reactivate accounts, publish opportunities and download activity reports.

Stay safe: BizBridge does not accept payments and never charges you to apply for an opportunity. Check people and organisations carefully before you exchange money or private information.
